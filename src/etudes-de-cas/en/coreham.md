## The context

Coreham, a **professional cleaning** company, had **no online presence**. The whole business relied on word of mouth and time-consuming cold outreach: hard to reach new clients, and a lot of time spent prospecting.

## The goal

Give Coreham **visibility on the web**, receive **inbound enquiries** directly from the site, and **cut down on cold outreach**.

## What I delivered

A complete **WordPress showcase site**:

- a presentation of the company and its cleaning **services**;
- the **service area**;
- a **contact form** for quote requests;
- a base of **local SEO** and a **Google Business Profile** to be found in sector-related searches.

All built on **WordPress with Elementor**, so the company can update its own content.

## How it went

Delivered in **two weeks**, in four steps:

1. **Understand the need** — conversations to pin down the business, the services and the target clients.
2. **Mockups** — a proposed structure and design, approved before any development.
3. **Build** — integration, content and the form, with sign-off at each step to move forward.
4. **Go live** — publishing the site and handing it over to the client.

## The result

The site lifted Coreham out of pure word of mouth: **contact enquiries doubled** thanks to the form, and the company now does **far less cold outreach** than before. It finally has a showcase it can show and keep alive.
