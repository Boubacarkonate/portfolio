## The context

ADEI France already had a site, but a **dated** one: an ageing design, a few **bugs**, and an online image that no longer reflected the company's professionalism.

## The goal

**Redesign** the site for a more **modern, clear and professional** image, while **respecting** ADEI France's existing **identity**.

## What I delivered

A **complete redesign** of the site on **WordPress**:

- a **new design**, more modern and more readable;
- **fixing the bugs** of the old site;
- adding **WordPress modules** to extend features;
- integrating a **WhatsApp Business chat** for direct contact;
- the **SEO basics**;
- a **blog section** to publish articles and sustain visibility.

All built on **WordPress with Elementor**, so the company keeps control of its content.

## The result

The site now matches **much better the image** ADEI France wants to convey: more modern, clearer, more professional — without losing its identity.
